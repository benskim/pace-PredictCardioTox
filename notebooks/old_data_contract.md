# Naming Convention

All artifact names, column names, identifiers, and schema fields
must use lower_snake_case.

Examples:

lead_agreement_score
beat_agreement_score
qt_variance_leads
t_end_uncertainty_ms

Capitalized names are explanatory only and are not valid schema names.


Critical Architecture Rule



The QTc Confidence Engine consists of five independent evidence domains:

Signal Quality Evidence
Delineation Evidence
Morphology Evidence
Measurement Agreement Evidence
Clinical Context Evidence

Each domain must be computable independently.

No domain may require features generated by another domain.

Independence Requirements
Domain 1 — Signal Quality (Notebook 02)

Purpose:

Evaluate raw ECG signal quality only.

Allowed Inputs:

ECG waveform
Sampling rate
ADC metadata

Allowed Feature Families:

Baseline Wander
High Frequency Noise
Powerline Interference
Clipping
Flatline Detection
Electrode Motion
Signal-to-Noise Ratio

Prohibited Inputs:

Beat annotations
QT measurements
Delineation outputs
Agreement metrics
Morphology outputs

Prohibited Features:

BSQI
WSQI
Lead Agreement
Beat Agreement
QT Variance
Measurement Repeatability
Internal Consistency

Signal Quality must remain completely measurement-agnostic.

Domain 2 — Delineation Validation (Notebook 03)

Purpose:

Evaluate uncertainty of boundary detection.

Allowed Inputs:

ECG waveform
Delineation algorithm outputs

Allowed Features:

P onset uncertainty
P offset uncertainty
QRS onset uncertainty
QRS offset uncertainty
T onset uncertainty
T end uncertainty
Boundary confidence

Prohibited Inputs:

QT measurements
Agreement metrics
Confidence model outputs
Domain 3 — T-Wave Morphology (Notebook 04)

Purpose:

Evaluate ambiguity of T-wave morphology.

Allowed Inputs:

ECG waveform
Delineated T-wave boundaries

Allowed Features:

T amplitude
T width
T slope
T symmetry
Biphasic flag
Flattened flag
Morphology cluster
T-end ambiguity score
Morphology confidence

Prohibited Inputs:

QT measurements
Agreement metrics
Confidence model outputs
Domain 4 — Measurement Reliability (Notebook 05)

Purpose:

Evaluate consistency of QT measurements.

Allowed Inputs:

QT measurements
Beat-level measurements
Lead-level measurements

Allowed Features:

BSQI
WSQI
Lead Agreement
Beat Agreement
QT Variance Across Leads
QT Variance Across Beats
Missing Lead Penalty
Repeatability Score
Internal Consistency Score

Prohibited Inputs:

Signal Quality Features from Notebook 02

Specifically prohibited:

bw_index
hfn_index
snr_db
clipping_ratio
flatline_ratio
electrode_motion_index
signal_quality_score

Rationale:

Measurement Reliability and Signal Quality must remain statistically independent evidence streams.

Domain 5 — Clinical Context

Purpose:

Capture clinical context affecting confidence.

Allowed Features:

Arrhythmia indicators
Rhythm classification
Diagnostic category
Dataset origin

Clinical features must not be derived from confidence outputs.

Confidence Feature Assembly (Notebook 09)

Notebook 09 is the first location where evidence domains may be merged.

Allowed Inputs:

signal_quality_features.parquet
delineation_features.parquet
twave_features.parquet
measurement_reliability.parquet
clinical_context.parquet

No earlier notebook may consume outputs from another evidence domain.

Dependency Graph (Mandatory)

Valid:

02 → 09

03 → 09

04 → 09

05 → 09

06 → 09

Invalid:

02 → 05

05 → 02

02 → 04

04 → 05

03 → 05

05 → 03

Any cross-domain dependency before Notebook 09 constitutes a contract violation.



Leakage Prevention Rule

Confidence targets must never appear upstream.

Forbidden:

absolute_qt_error_ms
expert_disagreement_ms
true_t_end_error_ms
confidence_probability
confidence_label

These variables may only exist inside Notebook 10 and later.

Confidence Modeling Principle

The confidence model learns from independent evidence streams.

Notebook 10 may discover that:

low SNR predicts low confidence
high lead disagreement predicts low confidence

However:

Signal Quality Notebook must never compute lead disagreement
Measurement Reliability Notebook must never compute SNR-derived scores

Evidence fusion occurs only in Notebook 09.
